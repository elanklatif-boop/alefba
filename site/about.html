Who made Alefba

A real person, a real reason, and a way to reach me.

Hi, I'm Elan, and I made Alefba.

I made it because I wanted a simple way for kids and families to learn to read and speak Persian, starting with the alphabet, and to hear how people really talk at home, not just how books write it.

Real voices

Many of the words in the app are recorded by me and my family, so you hear how people actually say them. The rest use a computer voice for now, and I'm adding more recordings every week.

Still growing

Alefba is new, and I'm improving it all the time based on what learners, parents and teachers tell me. If something is confusing, wrong or missing, I'd really like to know.

Parents can read how Alefba keeps children safe, including what it stores and how the AI talks work.

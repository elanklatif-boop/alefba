How to say I miss you in Persian

Delam barât tang shode: what it means, the polite form, jât khâli, and how to say you miss home.

To say I miss you in Persian, say delam barât tang shode (دلم برات تنگ شده). Literally it means “my heart has become tight for you.” To say it politely, or to more than one person, say delam barâtun tang shode (دلم براتون تنگ شده).

How do you say I miss you in Persian?

Say delam barât tang shode (دلم برات تنگ شده). Literally it means “my heart has become tight for you.” In writing it's delam barâye to tang shode ast.

Persian talks about missing someone with the heart: دل del is the heart, and تنگ tang means tight or narrow. Your heart gets tight when someone you love is far away.

  • دلم برات تنگ شدهdelam barât tang shode: I miss youTo one person you're close to: a parent, a friend, a cousin.
  • دلم براتون تنگ شدهdelam barâtun tang shode: I miss you (politely, or all of you)To grandparents, to a whole family, or to someone you'd call shomâ.
  • خیلی دلم برات تنگ شدهkheyli delam barât tang shode: I miss you so muchKheyli means very.
  • دلم برات یه ذره شدهdelam barât ye zarre shode: I miss you terriblyLiterally “my heart has shrunk to a tiny speck for you.” Warm and a little dramatic, for family and close friends.

Spoken and written

These are the everyday forms. In writing, the same sentence is دلم برای تو تنگ شده است delam barâye to tang shode ast: barât is the spoken form of barâye to (for you), and barâtun of barâye shomâ. The ast at the end is dropped when people talk, the same way many verbs get shorter.

What does jât khâli mean?

Jât khâli means “your place was empty.” People say it when you missed something good, like a family dinner or a trip: we missed you, we wish you'd been there.

  • جات خالیjât khâli: we missed you (your place was empty)When you tell someone about something good they missed: a party, a meal, a trip.
  • جاتون خالیjâtun khâli: the polite formTo someone older, or to several people.

It's a kind thing to say, so you'll often hear it with food: if someone shows you a photo of a big family dinner and says “jât khâli”, they're telling you they thought of you.

How do you say I miss home, or I miss Iran?

Delam barâye Irân tang shode. The same words work for a place or a thing: delam barâye khune tang shode means I miss home.

  • دلم برای خونه تنگ شدهdelam barâye khune tang shode: I miss homeKhune is the spoken form of khâne (house, home).
  • دلم برای ایران تنگ شدهdelam barâye Irân tang shode: I miss IranYou'll hear this one a lot from grandparents.
  • دلم تنگ شدهdelam tang shode: I miss (them, it, home)On its own it means you're missing someone or somewhere, and it can also mean feeling a bit homesick or low.

How do you reply?

The simplest reply is manam (منم, me too), or the full manam delam barât tang shode. To be warmer, add azizam (my dear): manam, azizam.

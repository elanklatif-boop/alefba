Who Alefba is for

Alefba is for children who hear Persian at home but can't yet read it or answer back with confidence, and for anyone starting from zero. Many families in the US, Canada, the UK and Europe want their children to talk with grandparents and cousins in Persian. Alefba is built for exactly that.

What your child learns

  • The Persian alphabet: all 32 letters, one at a time, with each letter's shapes, a word that uses it, and tracing practice.
  • 252 everyday words: family, food, school, greetings, days of the week, Nowruz and more, many recorded by a real Persian speaker, each with an example sentence.
  • Spoken Persian, not just book Persian: Alefba shows how a word is written in books (خانه, khâne) and how people really say it (خونه, khune), side by side.
  • Finglish to start: Persian words are shown in English letters too (like salâm), until your child can read the Persian script.
  • 48 short conversations: meet a new classmate, talk with a grandmother, set the haft-sin for Nowruz, land in Tehran, grouped by level.
  • 16 short stories: one at the end of every level, made from its words, about Sara, her little brother Nima and their cat Pishi. Tap any underlined word to see what it means.
  • A free Shab-e Yalda pack: the words for Yalda night, a story about Sara and Nima staying up late at Grandma's, and a picture game. It's free for everyone.
  • Pictures, games and stickers: pictures for the words so young children can learn before they read English, a memory game and a balloon game in each level, and a sticker for every level finished.
  • Grammar in small bites: fourteen short tips on how Persian works, like the little “-e” that joins words and how verbs change, each offered after the lessons it belongs with.
  • Spoken conversations (with a plan): the character speaks and your child answers out loud, with Slower, Show words and Hint buttons.
  • Made for families: several learners can share one device, each with their own progress and daily goal, and Alefba can be added to the home screen like an app.

Word meanings can be shown in English, French, German, Swedish or Chinese.

Practice talking with AI

In a talk, your child chats in Persian with a friendly character, such as a classmate or a grandmother, at their own level. The character helps when they're stuck and gently shows a better way to say things. Every talk is labeled AI, the chats aren't saved, and a parent signs in first. Every family gets 3 free talks. The alphabet and the first three levels are free; a plan opens all sixteen levels, the talks and speaking practice, from about $3.25 a month on the yearly plan. Is there a Duolingo for Persian?

As of 2026, Duolingo doesn't offer a Persian (Farsi) course, so many parents look for another way. Alefba is a Persian course made for children, free to start, focused on the everyday Persian spoken at home. Alefba isn't connected to Duolingo.

How it works

  • It runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to install and no account needed for the free lessons.
  • Short lessons, a few minutes each, with a daily goal and review of the words your child finds tricky.
  • Your child's name and progress stay on your own device, unless you turn on progress on every device (it's off until you do). Alefba has no ads and no tracking.

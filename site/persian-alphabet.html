The Persian alphabet

All 32 letters with their names, their sounds and a recording of each one, plus the few rules that make reading Persian easier.

The Persian (Farsi) alphabet has 32 letters and is written from right to left. It's based on the Arabic alphabet, with four extra letters for Persian sounds: پ (p), چ (ch), ژ (zh) and گ (g). Most letters join to the next one and change shape a little depending on where they are in a word, and short vowels are usually not written.

Which letters are only in Persian?

Four: پ pe (p), چ che (ch), ژ zhe (zh, the sound in “measure”) and گ gâf (g). The Arabic alphabet has no letters for these sounds, so Persian made new ones by adding dots or a line to Arabic ones.

پ is ب with three dots, چ is ج with three dots, ژ is ز with three dots, and گ is ک with a second line.

Which Persian letters don't join to the next letter?

Seven: ا د ذ ر ز ژ و. They join to the letter before them but never to the one after, so a small gap follows them.

That's why درخت (derakht, tree) looks like it's in three pieces: د and ر don't join forward, and خت is joined.

Why do Persian letters change shape?

Persian is always written joined up, like cursive. Most letters have a short shape when they join to the next letter and a full shape at the end of a word. For example, ب is بـ at the start of a word, ـبـ in the middle and ـب at the end. It's the same letter; only the joining changes.

Which Persian letters make the same sound?

Several sounds have more than one letter, because Persian kept the spelling of words that came from Arabic: s is ث س ص, z is ذ ز ض ظ, t is ت ط, h is ح ه, and gh is غ ق. You learn which one a word uses by learning the word.

Are vowels written in Persian?

The long vowels are written: â with ا, u with و and i with ی. The short vowels a, e and o are usually left out, so you learn them with each word. Children's books often show them with small marks above and below the letters.

The marks are called zebar (a), zir (e) and pish (o).

How long does it take to learn the Persian alphabet?

It depends on how often you practice. At two or three letters a day, you'll meet all 32 in two to three weeks. Reading smoothly takes longer, and comes from reading real words.

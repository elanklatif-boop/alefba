How does the taarof rally work?

Auntie keeps offering. Each time, a cup of tea slides toward you, and before it reaches you, you tap a polite reply. Never the same one twice in a row, and never a blunt one, or the tea spills. It gets faster each time. How long can you keep it going?

Your polite replies

These are the six things you can say back. They're real phrases you'll hear at any Iranian home. Tap ▶ to hear each one.

  • زحمت نکشیدzahmat nakeshid: Don't trouble yourself!
  • شرمندهsharmande: You're too kind!
  • مزاحم نمی‌شمmozâhem nemisham: I won't impose!
  • خواهش می‌کنمkhâhesh mikonam: Oh, please!
  • دستت درد نکنهdastet dard nakone: Thank you so much!
  • قربونت برمghorbunet beram: Thank you, dear!

Auntie's side of the rally is just as useful: befarmâ (here, have some), taârof nakon (don't be shy), ghâbel nadâre (it's nothing) and nush-e jân (enjoy).

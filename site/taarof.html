What happens in the taarof test?

Six everyday scenes, about two minutes in all. In each one someone offers you something, or insists, and you pick what to say from three answers. After each answer you see the Persian, how to say it, what it means, and why it's the right (or the wrong) thing to say.

What is taarof?

Taarof (تعارف) is the Persian etiquette of offering, refusing and insisting. A host offers, a guest politely says no, the host insists, and only then does the guest accept.

For Persian teachers

Give your students a fun way to practise Persian between classes. Make a free class, share its code, assign lessons and see who's practising.

In short: teacher mode is free. Students join your class from any phone, tablet or computer with a code, and don't need an email address or an account. The alphabet and the first 3 levels are free for every student, and a school plan ($49 a year) opens all 16 levels for a whole class.

How it works

  1. Make a class. Sign in with your email (we send you a code), then give the class a name, like Saturday beginners. You can have up to 20 classes, with up to 60 students in each.
  2. Share the code. Students open learnalefba.com, go to the Review tab, tap Join a class and type the code. The Copy an invite message button writes the message to send home for you.
  3. Assign lessons. Pick letters, word lessons, conversations or a whole level. Students see them at the top of their Lessons tab.
  4. See who's practising. For each student: how many of the last 7 days they practised, their streak, how many lessons they've done, and how many of your assigned lessons are finished.
  5. Open every level for the class (optional). With the school plan, everyone in the class gets all 16 levels for a year, so you can assign any lesson.

What your students practise

  • The alphabet: all 32 letters, one at a time, with each letter's shapes, a word that uses it, and tracing practice.
  • 252 everyday words in 16 levels: family, food, school, animals, colours, Nowruz and more, with sound for every word and an example sentence.
  • Spoken and book Persian side by side: how people say a word at home (خونه, khune) and how books write it (خانه, khâne).
  • Finglish as a support: words are also shown in English letters (like salâm) until students can read the script.
  • 16 short stories, one for every level, made from its words. Students tap any underlined word to see what it means.
  • A free Shab-e Yalda pack: 15 Yalda words (watermelon, pomegranate, nuts, candles, Hafez), a story about Yalda night at Grandma's house and a game. It's free for every student, with or without a plan, and you can assign it like any lesson.
  • Pictures and games for young learners: pictures for the words, a memory game and a balloon game in each level, and a sticker for every level finished.
  • Little mode, for ages 4 to 6: for children who can't read yet. They tap a big letter to hear it, see a picture word that starts with it, and trace it with a finger. A short voice says what to do, and every letter traced earns a star.
  • Printable tracing sheets: pick the letters your class is learning and print a page for each, with dotted letters to trace, the letter's joined shapes, and a word with a picture. Open them from the Letters tab in the Library, or from teacher mode.
  • Practice conversations with friendly AI characters, at each student's level. Every family gets 3 free talks; more come with a plan.
  • Meanings in five languages: English, French, German, Swedish or Chinese.

Alefba is made for children aged about 7 to 14, with a little mode for ages 4 to 6, and it has an adult version too, with its own conversations, so teen and adult classes can use it as well.

Privacy, for schools

  • Students don't need an email address or an account to do the lessons or join a class.
  • A class sees only the first name or nickname a student types when joining, and their progress: lessons done, days practised and streak. Nothing from the AI talks is ever shared with a class.
  • On a child's device, a grown-up says yes before the child joins a class.
  • Students can leave a class at any time, which deletes their progress from it straight away. Deleting a class deletes everything in it.
  • Alefba has no ads and no tracking cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects nothing that identifies a student. The app also keeps daily totals, such as how many lessons were finished, with nothing about who anyone is.

School plan: $49 a yearOne class, all 16 levels for every student in it (up to 60), on any device. Paid once; it doesn't renew by itself.
Family plan: from $5 a monthOr $39 a year. All 16 levels, plus the AI talks and speaking practice, for one family.

With the school plan, families don't need their own plan for the lessons. The AI talks and speaking practice aren't part of it, because they need a grown-up to sign in: families who want them can add a family plan. You get the school plan from your class in teacher mode, and you can add another year near the end.

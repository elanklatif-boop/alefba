What is Shab-e Yalda?

Shab-e Yalda (شب یلدا) is the longest night of the year, the winter solstice, around 21 December. For thousands of years, Persian families have stayed up together that night to welcome the sun, which from the next day stays a little longer.

The table has watermelon and pomegranates, red like the dawn, with nuts, dried fruit and sweets. Grandparents tell stories, and someone opens the book of Hafez, the great poet of Shiraz, to read a fâl for each person's wish. In Iran, in Afghanistan and Tajikistan, and in Persian families everywhere, it's one of the warmest nights of the year.

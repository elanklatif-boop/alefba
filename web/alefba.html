All 32 Persian letters. Persian is read from right to left, and most letters change shape depending on where they sit in a word: on their own, at the start, in the middle and at the end. Seven letters (ا د ذ ر ز ژ و) never join to the letter after them. Tap a letter to hear it, or switch to writing and trace it with your finger. The green bar shows how well you know it.
